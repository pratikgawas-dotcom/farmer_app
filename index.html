How Farmer App Eliminates Middlemen

Re-engineering food logistics to reward the growers

❌ The Traditional Model

  • 1. Farmer sells harvest in Mandi at distress pricing
  • 2. Commission Agent charges 6-10%
  • 3. Transport Broker adds markup
  • 4. Wholesaler stores in cold storage (+20%)
  • 5. City Retailer/Supermarket adds +35-50% markup
  • Result: Farmer gets ~₹14, Consumer pays ₹45!

✅ The Farmer App Model

  • 1. Farmer posts live harvest directly with transparent rates
  • 2. Consumer orders directly from verified local growers
  • 3. 100% payment transfers directly to the farmer's bank/UPI
  • 4. Produce is picked fresh at dawn and delivered fast
  • 5. Zero commissions, zero broker fees
  • Result: Farmer gets ₹28 (+100%), Consumer pays ₹28 (Save 38%)!

By utilizing digital UPI and direct farm-to-consumer logistics, farmers earn sustainable livelihoods, invest in organic farming, and consumers enjoy harvested-today nutritional freshness.
